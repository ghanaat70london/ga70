FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Ghana at 70 UK: London to mark seventy years of Ghanaian independence with three days at Banking Hall

London, 17 November 2026. On 6 March 2027 Ghana turns seventy. Guests from the UK, Europe, North America and Ghana will mark it with Ghana at 70 UK, held in London, The Platinum Jubilee Celebrations: three days at Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, in the City of London.

Friday 5 March, The Royal Jubilee Gala. Independence Eve. Fontomfrom drums and a red carpet at 18:30, a Ghanaian inspired dinner, live highlife and, at midnight, the whole hall rising for the moment Ghana became independent in 1957. A seated dinner at long tables. Black Tie, ball gown or Kente.

Saturday 6 March, the Ghana Day Festival. Independence Day itself. A DJ all day, Ghanaian and African businesses, five practical talks on land, money, wills, pensions and business, and the anniversary moment at 11:00 with drumming and a reading of the declaration of independence. All ages welcome.

Sunday 7 March, Women of the Motherland. Mothering Sunday falls the day after the anniversary. A family afternoon tea open to every woman, whatever her heritage, and her family, with the Wall of Mothers, where every mother named by a guest is celebrated aloud, Voices of the Motherland and a family portrait. Talks and masterclasses are being shaped with guests and are not yet confirmed.

Ghana at 70 UK is created by joint founders Asantewaa-Gloria Ayim and Adwoa Agyemang, who together opened the new Ghana Passport Office in Aldgate East and founded Accra Konnect.

"Seventy years is not an anniversary. It is a homecoming," say Asantewaa and Adwoa. "Bring your mother. Bring your children. Bring the friend who has never been. There is a seat at our table for every one of you."

Tickets: Royal Jubilee Gala GBP 200, Ghana Day Festival and Women of the Motherland tickets in the New Year. Book at https://ghanaat70uk.com.

About Ghana at 70 UK

Ghana at 70 UK, The Platinum Jubilee Celebrations, marks seventy years of Ghanaian independence with three days at Banking Hall in the City of London, from Friday 5 to Sunday 7 March 2027: The Royal Jubilee Gala, the Ghana Day Festival and Women of the Motherland. It is created by joint founders Asantewaa-Gloria Ayim and Adwoa Agyemang.

About the founders

Asantewaa-Gloria Ayim and Adwoa Agyemang are joint founders of Ghana at 70 UK and of Accra Konnect, and together opened the new Ghana Passport Office in Aldgate East. Asantewaa has spent twenty years delivering events at the highest level, for Pirelli, the Spanish national football team and Champions League clubs. Adwoa founded Ghanaian Londoners Network in 2009, has delivered more than thirty community events, spent nine years in PR and media in Accra and sits on the Ghana Nigeria Business Council Executive Committee.
