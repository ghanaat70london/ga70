Exhibitor questions

Before you apply.

The questions exhibitors ask most.

Register to exhibit
Who visits the Ghana Day Festival?

Families, friends and the whole community, every one holding a ticket. Every visitor has chosen to spend the day with Ghanaian brands, so the people at your stand arrive ready to buy.

Will the hall be comfortable?

Yes. Entry is managed so the hall never holds more than its safe limit at one time, and that limit is confirmed by the venue. We report the real number to every exhibitor after the day.

Can more than one business from my category exhibit?

Yes, up to a set number in each category, so the floor holds a balanced mix and every stand is seen. When a category is full, new businesses join its waiting list.

How much does a stand cost?

Stand options and full details go to everyone who registers their interest to exhibit. Every stand includes its build.

Can I exhibit on Friday or Sunday?

Not on Friday: the hall is the Gala. On Sunday, yes, with twelve stands in the side aisles. Six are Motherland Market stands. Six are Resource Village stands for organisations that support women, arranged through our partnership team.

Why are there fewer stands on Sunday?

On Sunday the hall is a dining room. Every family is seated for afternoon tea, so stands only go where no table can: the two side aisles between the columns. No table is moved and no seat is lost for a stand.

Can I sponsor as well as exhibit?

Yes. Sponsorship sits with our partners and can include a stand. Request the sponsorship pack.

Exhibitor rules

The standards that keep the hall beautiful.

Read the exhibitor rules
Curation
Every exhibitor is approved by category. Products must match the application
Market stand food
Sealed, labelled products to take home only, with allergen information and registration with your local authority as a food business
Insurance
Public liability insurance is required. Send your certificate with your final details
Electrics
Electrical items must be safety tested and suitable for indoor use
Payments
Bring a card reader with its own mobile data. Do not rely on venue wifi
Build and pack down
Stands are staffed from doors to close. Nothing is packed away before 17:00
Property, money and legal
Estate agents, developers, financial and legal exhibitors must hold the registrations the law requires and send proof with their application, so our guests are protected
The Gala
Exhibiting does not include the Royal Jubilee Gala. Stand teams on site on Friday evening need a Gala ticket, at the exhibitor rate
Cancellation
Stand fees cannot be refunded once paid. Your stand can be transferred to another approved business
