Exhibitor questions

Before you apply.

The questions exhibitors ask most.

Apply for a stand
Who visits the Ghana Day Festival?

Families, friends and the whole community, every one holding a ticket. We are planning for around 500 visitors through the day, the level a comparable community show of forty exhibitors has drawn. Every visitor has chosen to spend the day with Ghanaian brands, so the people at your stand arrive ready to buy.

How many visitors will there be?

We are planning for around 500 visitors across the day. Entry is managed so the hall never holds more than its safe limit at one time, and that limit is confirmed by the venue. We report the real number to every exhibitor after the day.

Can more than one business from my category exhibit?

Yes. Every category is open to more than one exhibitor. We plan the floor with care, so similar stands have space between them and every stand is seen.

How much does a stand cost?

A market stand is GBP 300 + VAT (GBP 360 in total) and a feature stand is GBP 600 + VAT (GBP 720 in total). Both prices include the stand build.

Can I exhibit on Friday or Sunday?

Not on Friday: the hall is the Gala. On Sunday, yes, with twelve stands in the side aisles. Six are Motherland Market stands, at GBP 150 + VAT (GBP 180 in total). Six are Resource Village stands for organisations that support women, arranged through our partnership team. See Saturday and Sunday compared.

Why are there fewer stands on Sunday?

On Sunday the hall is a dining room. Every family is seated for afternoon tea, so stands only go where no table can: the two side aisles between the columns. No table is moved and no seat is lost for a stand.

Can I sponsor as well as exhibit?

Yes. Sponsorship sits with our partners and can include a stand. Request the sponsorship pack.

Public liability insurance is required. Send your certificate with your final details
Electrics
Electrical items must be safety tested and suitable for indoor use
Payments
