Independence Day

Born in Kumasi. Born in Birmingham. Born Ghanaian.

Seventy years to the day, and the whole family under one roof.

The aunties who argue about the jollof. The cousins who switch to Twi or Ga when they want to keep a secret. The friend from work who has never been, and will never miss it again. Families travelling in from Milton Keynes, Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Glasgow, Cardiff, Belfast and Dublin, from across Europe and America, and from Ghana itself.

Continue the story

First the exhibition: up to forty companies that want to reach the Ghanaian diaspora, with fixed 10 minute appointments with the exhibitors you choose. Then a DJ all day, the dance circle, games and the Ghana at 70 Quiz. At noon the whole hall stops for the drums and a reading of the declaration of independence. Then the music starts again.

Today Ghana is seventy. Today Ghana is ours.

15:00 · The Ghana at 70 Fashion Show

Seventy years of Ghanaian craft, on the runway.

Led by our Fashion Partner, the Accra fashion house Quophi Akotuah, Ghanaian fashion houses bring the arts of Ghana to the runway at Banking Hall: Kente from the loom, Krobo glass beads, Adinkra stamped by hand, and the designers dressing modern Ghana.

  1. The runwayGhanaian fashion houses at 15:00 on the floor of the hall, organised by our Fashion Partner Quophi Akotuah.
  2. The Fashion ExhibitionDesigners, weavers, bead makers, jewellers and artists at their stands all day. See it, touch it, order it.
  3. Order before you arriveThe collections open online on 1 February 2027, with a discount for ticket holders. Order by 20 February and your piece is adjusted to fit, ready to collect on Saturday.

Before you reserve
Is everything confirmed?

Yes. All three days are at Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, in the City of London, moments from the Bank of England. If we ever cancel, every ticket is refunded in full within 14 days. It is in our terms.

Who is behind it?

The team that produced the opening of the Ghana Passport Office in Aldgate East. Watch the film.

Where can I celebrate Ghana Independence Day in London?

At the Ghana Day Festival, Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, on Saturday 6 March 2027 from 11:00 to 18:00. Seventy years to the day since 6 March 1957, with up to forty exhibitors of every kind, fixed 10 minute appointments, a DJ, dancing and the whole community in one hall.

Is there dancing?

All day. The DJ plays highlife, hiplife and Afrobeats from 11:00, and the dance circle is open to every age. Come and go with your wristband.

Can I join the games?

Yes. Entry is included with your ticket. Enter now with the games form on the Ghana Day page, or at the welcome desk on the day.

Can we come as a team?

Yes. A quiz team booking is six tickets. Choose Quiz team, 6 tickets when you book.

Is the programme confirmed?

The programme shown is the plan we are building to. Performers, speakers and timings are announced as each one is contracted, and every booked guest hears first.
