Before you book
Is everything confirmed?

Yes. All three days are at Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, in the City of London. If we ever cancel, every ticket is refunded in full within 14 days. It is in our terms.

Who is behind it?

The team that produced the opening of the Ghana Passport Office in Aldgate East. Watch the film.

Where can I celebrate Ghana Independence Day in London?

At the Ghana Day Festival, Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, on Saturday 6 March 2027, 08:30 to 17:00. Seventy years to the day since 6 March 1957.

Can my business exhibit?

Yes. Every business is welcome. Register your interest to exhibit and we return with the full details.

Can we come as a team?

Yes. A group booking is six tickets: 6 x GBP 50 = GBP 300. Choose Group of six when you book.

Is the programme confirmed?

The programme shown is the plan we are building to. Performers, speakers and timings are announced as each one is contracted, and every booked guest hears first.
