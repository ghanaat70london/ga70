Independence Eve

Midnight, 6 March. Seventy years free.

In 1957 our grandparents stood under the warm night sky of Accra and waited for midnight.

On Independence Eve we wait for it together again, in Kente and gold, with guests from every corner of the UK, Europe, North America and Ghana, in a grand banking hall in the City of London.

Continue the story

Fontomfrom drums at the door. A Ghanaian inspired dinner at long tables. Live highlife and a dance floor. Then, as the clock turns, the whole hall rises, glasses high, for the moment Ghana became free, seventy years on.

Arrive in your finest. One night, seventy years in the making.

The setting

Banking Hall, City of London.

A Grade II listed Art Deco banking hall on Cornhill, moments from the Bank of England and the Royal Exchange. For one night, the grandest dining room in the City belongs to Ghana.

Address14 Cornhill, London EC3V 3ND
HeritageGrade II listed 1930s Art Deco, once a Lloyds Bank headquarters
The roomA seated dinner at long tables
Nearest stationBank, about two minutes on foot
Getting there

Bank Central, Northern, Waterloo and City lines and the DLR, about two minutes on foot. Monument District and Circle lines. Mainline rail Cannon Street, Fenchurch Street and Liverpool Street, with the Elizabeth line at Liverpool Street.

Before you reserve
Is everything confirmed?

Yes. All three days are at Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, in the City of London, moments from the Bank of England. If we ever cancel, every ticket is refunded in full within 14 days. It is in our terms.

Who is behind it?

The team that produced the opening of the Ghana Passport Office in Aldgate East. Watch the film.

Is this the Ghana Independence dinner and dance in London?

Yes. The Royal Jubilee Gala is the black tie dinner dance on the eve of Ghana Independence Day: a Ghanaian inspired dinner, a live band playing highlife and Afrobeats, the dance floor until the last dance, and the midnight toast as 6 March 2027 begins.

What do I wear?

Black tie and ball gowns, with Kente welcome. This is a ball. What to wear

Can I book a table for my group?

Yes. A table of ten is GBP 1,800: ten guests seated together at the long tables, for the price of nine: 9 x GBP 200. Choose Table of ten when you reserve.

Is the programme confirmed?

The programme shown is the plan we are building to. Performers, speakers and timings are announced as each one is contracted, and every booked guest hears first.
