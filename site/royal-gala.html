Independence Eve

Midnight, 6 March. Seventy years free.

In 1957 our grandparents stood under the warm night sky of Accra and waited for midnight.

On Independence Eve we wait for it together again, in Kente and gold, with guests from every corner of the UK, Europe, North America and Ghana, in London.

Live highlife, and a dance floor for the songs of Amakye Dede, the Iron Boy.

Before you book
Is everything confirmed?

Yes. All three days are at Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, in the City of London. If we ever cancel, every ticket is refunded in full within 14 days. It is in our terms.

Who is behind it?

The team that produced the opening of the Ghana Passport Office in Aldgate East. Watch the film.

Is this the Ghana Independence dinner and dance in London?

Yes. The Royal Jubilee Gala is the black tie dinner dance on the eve of Ghana Independence Day: a Ghanaian inspired dinner, a live band playing highlife and Afrobeats, the dance floor until the last dance, and the midnight toast as 6 March 2027 begins.

Why is it called the Royal Jubilee Gala?

In Ghana, royalty is living culture, and at a great celebration every guest is honoured as royalty. Ghanaian traditional rulers are invited and received with protocol. Ghana at 70 UK is not connected with, or endorsed by, the British Royal Family.

What do I wear?

Black tie and ball gowns, with Kente welcome. This is a ball. What to wear

Can I book a table for my group?

Yes. A table of ten is GBP 1,800: ten guests seated together at the long tables, for the price of nine: 9 x GBP 200. Choose Table of ten when you book.

Is the programme confirmed?

The programme shown is the plan we are building to. Performers, speakers and timings are announced as each one is contracted, and every booked guest hears first.
