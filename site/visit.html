Every route, step by step

From Bank station, in four steps

  1. Follow the signs to Exit 4On the platform signs it reads Exit 4: Cornhill, Leadenhall Market, Lloyds. Exit 3, Royal Exchange, brings you up just as close.
  2. Come up on Bank JunctionThe great columned front of the Royal Exchange and the Bank of England stand around you.
  3. Find CornhillIt is the street running between the Royal Exchange and the domed corner building at Number 1 Cornhill.
  4. Walk a few doors alongBanking Hall is at Number 14, on the side opposite the Royal Exchange. Our hosts in green and gold meet you at the door.
Your route

Wherever you are starting from.

By Underground

  • Bank Central, Northern and Waterloo and City lines and the DLR. Exit 4, then one minute on foot.
  • Monument District and Circle lines. Walk up King William Street to Bank Junction and turn right into Cornhill. About six minutes.
  • Waterloo and City line Waterloo to Bank in about four minutes. It runs Monday to Friday only, so it serves the Gala but not the weekend.

From a London terminal

  • Euston, Kings Cross, St Pancras Northern line, Bank branch, direct to Bank. About 10 minutes.
  • London Bridge Northern line, one stop to Bank.
  • Liverpool Street Central line, one stop to Bank, or about 10 minutes on foot along Bishopsgate and Cornhill.
  • Paddington Elizabeth line to Liverpool Street, then as above.
  • Cannon Street and Fenchurch Street About six to eight minutes on foot.

From the airports

  • London City DLR direct to Bank. About 25 minutes.
  • Heathrow Elizabeth line to Liverpool Street, then the Central line one stop to Bank.
  • Gatwick Thameslink to London Bridge, then the Northern line one stop to Bank.
  • Stansted Stansted Express to Liverpool Street, then the Central line one stop to Bank.
  • Eurostar St Pancras, then the Northern line direct to Bank.

By taxi or car

  • Taxi Ask for Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill. On weekdays Bank Junction is open to buses and cycles only during the day, so ask your driver to come along Cornhill from the Gracechurch Street end.
  • Parking The venue has none and City parking is scarce. Book a space in advance through a parking app.
  • Charges The venue is inside the Congestion Charge zone and the ULEZ. Check the hours on the TfL website before you drive.

Going home after the Gala

  • Night Tube On Friday night the Central line runs all night through Bank. The Northern line Night Tube runs on the Charing Cross branch, not through Bank.
  • Night buses Many routes serve Bank through the night.
  • Taxis Our hosts will point you to the pick up point on Cornhill as the evening closes.

Step free

  • Bank station Lifts from the Cannon Street entrance give step free access from street to train on the Northern line, the DLR and the Waterloo and City line. About five minutes on foot to Cornhill.
  • Inside Banking Hall The event is held over two floors joined by stairs. Read the access guide before you book.

For any journey, the TfL journey planner shows live times on the day.

Before you travel

What you need to enter the UK.

It depends on the passport you travel on, not where you live. Every traveller, children included, needs their own.

When to apply, your visa letter and when to arrive
  1. When to applyVisa applications open three months before you travel, so from early December 2026. A decision usually takes about three weeks. Apply by mid January 2027. Processing times.
  2. A letter for your visaTicket holders can ask us for a letter confirming the booking and the dates. It confirms the booking only, and is not an invitation or sponsorship.
  3. When to arriveComing from abroad for the Gala? Arrive by Thursday 4 March 2027, so the evening begins rested.

Checked on gov.uk on 22 November 2026. The Home Office decides every application. Ghana at 70 UK does not pay for, sponsor or guarantee any visa.

From Accra to Banking Hall, with ease.

Adansi Travels is our travel partner in Ghana. Guests and exhibitors coming from the Motherland are guided through the UK visa application and flights to London, at their own cost. We look after your stay from the moment you land.

  2. Your flightsAccra to London, booked for the weekend you want.

Access

Please read before you book.

Banking Hall is a Grade II listed building. We want every guest to arrive knowing exactly what to expect.

The access guide
  1. ArrivalThe entrance is at 14 Cornhill, one minute from Bank station. Step free routes through Bank station start at the Cannon Street entrance. Hosts meet every guest at the door.
  2. InsideThe event is held over two floors joined by stairs only.
  3. SeatingEvery guest has a seat at the Gala and on Sunday. On Saturday, seating is available around the hall.
  4. Children, pushchairs and buggiesAll ages are welcome, with children accompanied by an adult. The two floors are joined by stairs, so pushchairs and buggies cannot be accommodated.
  5. CloakroomFully staffed on all three days, so you do not carry coats or bags on the stairs.
  6. Before you bookCall Asantewaa on +44 7824 555307 and we will tell you honestly what is possible.
