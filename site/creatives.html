The Creative Showcase

Seventy Years in Frame.

Ghana at 70, told in photography, film and visual art. Seventy photographs, one for every year since 1957, in seven chapters, with a short film of seven decades and seven artists answering them today.

  1. 1957 to 1966FreedomMidnight at the Old Polo Grounds, the Black Star rising and the studio portraits of a new nation.
  2. 1967 to 1976StyleHighlife nights, Kente and the first great wave of Ghanaian fashion and studio photography.
  3. 1977 to 1986ResilienceHard years, carried with dignity, the families who built new lives in London, and the highlife of Amakye Dede, the Iron Boy.
  4. 1987 to 1996Return of the voteThe Fourth Republic, burger highlife and the diaspora finding its voice in Britain.
  5. 1997 to 2006New voicesHiplife, new artists and a generation born between Accra and London.
  6. 2007 to 2016The world watchesThe Black Stars on the world stage and Ghanaian creativity crossing every border.
  7. 2017 to 2027The next frameThe Year of Return, Afrobeats everywhere, and the photographs taken in the hall on Independence Day itself.

Friday: the Walk of Seventy Years at the Gala. Saturday: the gallery from 08:30 and the Creative Showcase at 14:00 on the main stage. Sunday: the gallery all day.

Three ways of seeing

Photography, film and visual art.

  1. Seventy photographsPhotographyOne image for every year from 1957 to 2027, from licensed archives, family albums and photographers working today, each with its year and its credit.
  2. Seven minutesFilmOne minute for each decade, archive and new footage together, shown at the Gala and on the big screen at the Creative Showcase.
  3. Seven artistsVisual artSeven living artists, Ghanaian or of Ghanaian descent, each answering one decade with a new work. Every artist named, every work for sale.

In the spotlight

TwinsDntBeg

Seventy Years in Frame.

Twin brothers Emmanuel and Samuel Appiah Gyan, the Ghanaian photography and film duo behind Swag of Africa Studios, curate the photography exhibition of Ghana at 70 UK. In November 2026 they announced a collaboration with Canon to develop the next generation of African photographers, filmmakers and creators, and that same spirit runs through the creative sessions on Independence Day. Their work with Rocky Dawuni on Rise was nominated for Music Film of the Year at the 2025 Black Stars International Film Festival.

Ghana on Screen · Saturday 6 March 2027 · 15:00

Films from home, for the diaspora.

Documentaries and short films by Ghanaian filmmakers and filmmakers of Ghanaian descent, made in Ghana and across the diaspora, screened in the Talks Room on Independence Day, with the filmmakers joining from Accra and London. Home and the diaspora, in one room.

  1. The filmsGhana in its own wordsDocumentaries and short films about the people, places, history and future of Ghana, up to 20 minutes each.
  2. The conversationAccra and London togetherAfter the screening, the filmmakers answer questions from the hall, in person or live from Ghana.
  3. Open to allWith your Ghana Day ticketEvery Ghana Day guest is welcome. Filmmakers submit with the form below.
Selection

Selected by our curators.

Every artist, photographer and filmmaker is reviewed by our curators before taking part.

How selection works
  1. Step 1

    Register

    Tell us what you make and how you want to take part, with a link to your work.

  2. Step 2

    Curators review

    Ghanaian creatives and creatives of Ghanaian descent first. Quality, story, professionalism and the rights to show the work.

  3. Step 3

    A short call

    Shortlisted creatives meet the curators by video.

  4. Step 4

    Your agreement

    Credit, rights, conduct and timings in writing, before anything is announced.

  5. Step 5

    Accreditation

    Your name on the programme and your pass for the day.
