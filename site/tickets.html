How booking works

Booked in two minutes.

  1. Secure checkoutCheckout is run by Ticket Tailor and paid through Stripe, in GBP. Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay where your device offers them. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch this website.
  2. Booking for a groupEnter each guest by name at checkout. Every guest receives a ticket with their own QR code.
  3. Your ticketsThey arrive by email at once, with a PDF attached. Forward each guest their own ticket, or print the PDF. Keep your code private: each code admits one guest, once.
  4. Dietary and access needsFor the Gala we ask for dietary needs at checkout, and anything that would make your arrival easier, so the kitchen and our hosts are ready for you.
  5. Your choice on newsOne optional box at checkout, left unticked. Tick it only if you would like our news.
  6. RefundsAll sales are final and tickets are transferable to another guest. If we cancel, every ticket is refunded in full. Read our booking terms and privacy notice.
  7. Help with your bookingCall Asantewaa on +44 7824 555307 or email hello@ghanaat70uk.com.
