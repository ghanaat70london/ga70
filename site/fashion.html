Our Fashion Partner

Quophi Akotuah Ghana.

A luxury fashion house established in Accra in 2015 by Michael Addo, known as Quophi Michaels. Bold, artistic couture for women and men: bridal and red carpet gowns, tailoring and a growing ready to wear line.

From borrowed machines to building a brand that now dresses women and men who inspire nations.
