General

What is Ghana at 70 UK?

The global celebration of the Ghana 70th Platinum Jubilee: three separate events in London, for guests from across the UK, Europe, the Americas and Ghana, each with its own ticket, marking seventy years of Ghanaian independence: the Royal Jubilee Gala on Friday 5 March 2027, the Ghana Day Festival on Saturday 6 March 2027, and Women of the Motherland on Sunday 7 March 2027.

Can I attend if I am not Ghanaian?

Of course. Ghana at 70 UK is for everyone. Guests of every background and every heritage are warmly welcome on all three days.

Can I come to just one day?

Yes. Each day is ticketed separately and is complete on its own.

How is the programme timed?

Every day is planned to a published running order, sent to ticket holders before the event, so you can relax and enjoy it.

Is there a virtual option?

No. Ghana at 70 UK is a celebration held in person.

How do I book?

Choose your event on the Tickets page and book. Bookings are open, only on this website, and prices include all fees.

Tickets and payment

When can I book?

Now, only on this website. A table of ten is GBP 1,800, ten guests for the price of nine (9 x GBP 200).

Can I get a refund?

No. All sales are final and tickets cannot be refunded. If you cannot attend, transfer your ticket to someone else at no extra cost by emailing us their name before 17:00 on the day before. If we cancel the event, you are refunded in full.

Is there a prize draw?

Yes. Every ticket holder is entered into the Mefie hamper draw, and anyone can enter free, with no purchase, at ghanaat70uk.com/draw. One entry per person, the same chance either way. Entries close at 23:59 on Sunday 28 February 2027, and the winner is drawn on Independence Day, Saturday 6 March 2027.

I am not Ghanaian. Can I come?

Yes, and you are very welcome. Ghana at 70 UK is for everyone, whatever your heritage. Bring your colleagues, neighbours and friends. On Friday guests wear Black Tie, ball gown or Kente, and the dress code page shows how to wear Kente and print with confidence.

I need a UK visa. Where do I start?

Download our free visa and ETA checklist. It takes you step by step: what to gather, what your bank statements must show, ready letters and the online form. Every cost of your trip is yours: Ghana at 70 UK does not sponsor any visa or traveller and is not responsible for any cost. Download the checklist.

I live in Europe or the Nordics on a Ghanaian passport. What do I need?

A UK Standard Visitor visa, GBP 135, even with a residence permit or permanent residence. Apply online from early December 2026, give your fingerprints at the visa application centre where you live, and allow about three weeks. EU, Swiss, Norwegian and Icelandic passport holders need an ETA, GBP 20. Visas and travel.

I live outside the UK. Can I come?

Yes, and we would love you to. Guests are coming from across Europe, North America and Ghana. Book online in GBP from anywhere in the world. Most visitors from Europe and North America need a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) before they fly: apply at gov.uk/eta. Visitors from Ghana need a UK visitor visa: see Plan your journey. Our Stay team books hotels near Bank station.

Can I pay by bank transfer?

Yes. Choose bank transfer when you book and we send our account details with your booking reference. Payment must arrive within 24 hours, or the booking is released. Tickets are issued once payment arrives.

Can I add extras or host a private table?

Yes. Once your tickets are paid you can add private parties, celebration packages and portrait packages. Extras are priced on request.

Where should I book?

Only on this website, through the Book now buttons. Tickets bought anywhere else are not valid.

The Royal Jubilee Gala

Why is it called the Royal Jubilee Gala?

In Ghana, royalty is living culture. Kings, chiefs and queen mothers lead their communities today, and Kente was first woven as the cloth of kings. In Ghana, and across much of Africa, a great celebration is a royal occasion, and every guest is honoured as royalty: dressed in their finest, welcomed with drums and seated with dignity. That is the spirit of the evening. Ghanaian traditional rulers are invited and, when they attend, are received with protocol. Whoever you are and wherever you are from, come as royalty. Ghana at 70 UK is not connected with, or endorsed by, the British Royal Family. The Platinum Jubilee marks seventy years of Ghana as an independent nation.

What is the dress code?

Black tie and ball gowns, with Kente welcome. Miniature decorations may be worn.

Can I book for a group of ten?

Yes. A table of ten is GBP 1,800: ten guests seated together at the long tables, for the price of nine: 9 x GBP 200. Choose Table of ten when you book.

Are there awards?

No. Ghana at 70 UK is a celebration of Ghana, not an awards night. There are no awards, prizes or judging on any day.

Ghana Day Festival

Can I bring children?

Yes. All ages are welcome on all three days. Children must be accompanied by an adult at all times, and every guest, adult or child, holds a ticket. The event is held over two floors joined by stairs, so pushchairs and buggies cannot be accommodated.

Is there food and drink?

Saturday is a festival, not a meal. The bar sells drinks and snacks all day, card only. Food is not included in the ticket, and food and drink cannot be brought in. Over 18s only at the bar, and we follow Challenge 25.

Is anything on Saturday evening?

No. The festival closes at 17:00.

Women of the Motherland

Who is Sunday for?

Every woman, whatever her heritage, with her family and friends. Mothers, fathers, sons, daughters and grandparents are all welcome at family tables.

Does everyone pay the same?

Yes. One ticket covers afternoon tea, the Wall of Mothers, the toolkit, a family portrait and the networking hour, whether or not you drink alcohol.

Is there an age limit?

All ages welcome, as on every day. Children accompanied by an adult at all times. Every guest needs a ticket.

Is there more than one sitting?

No. There is one sitting and the whole room is together all day. Doors open at 11:00 and we close at 18:30.

Venue and access

Where is the venue?

All three days are at Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, London EC3V 3ND, in the City of London.

I do not live in London. Can you arrange my stay?

Yes. We book a hotel close to the venue or within your budget, plus airport and station transfers. Send a request from the Plan your journey page and receive your options within one working day.

Is the venue accessible?

Banking Hall is a historic building and the event is held over two floors connected by stairs only, so the event may not be suitable for guests who cannot use stairs. Please call us before booking and we will tell you honestly what is possible. See the access guide.

Will I be photographed?

Photography takes place throughout the weekend and portraits are delivered within seven days. If you prefer not to be photographed, tell a host on arrival.
