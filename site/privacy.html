Your details, handled with care.

Last updated 22 November 2026. This notice follows the UK General Data Protection Regulation, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.

1. Who we are

INTEROPA Holidays Ltd is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice.

  1. Registered in England and Wales, company number 01190602, established in 1974.
  2. Registered office: C/O Citycas, Room 540, Linen Hall, 162 to 168 Regent Street, London W1B 5TF.
  3. Privacy contact: hello@ghanaat70uk.com, with Privacy in the subject line.

2. What we collect

  1. When you book: your name, email and mobile, the tickets you choose, how you pay, the name of a guest if the ticket is a gift, and your acceptance of the terms of sale. Card details are entered on the secure pages of our payment provider. We never see or store your card number.
  2. When you send a form: your name and email, and anything else you choose to add, such as your mobile, party size, how you heard about us, the Sunday sessions you would like and your message.
  3. For organisations: for partners, sponsors, exhibitors, speakers, press and anyone who contacts us to reach the diaspora, your name, role, organisation, sector, website and the details of your enquiry or application.
  4. Access and dietary needs: only if you choose to tell us, so that we can look after you on the day. Where this reveals health information, we use it only for that purpose and only with your explicit consent.
  5. The Wall of Mothers: the name you ask us to display and read aloud on Sunday 7 March 2027.
  6. The Mefie hamper draw: your name and email, whether you enter through a ticket or the free entry route.
  7. Volunteers: your contact details, availability and the roles you would like.
  8. How you reached us: the page you arrived on, the website or advert that referred you and any campaign tag in the link.
  9. At the event: your ticket scan at the door. Photographers and film crews work throughout the three days, so you may appear in photographs and film. The venue may operate CCTV for safety, under its own privacy notice.

4. Who helps us

We share your details only with the providers who run the service for us, and only what each one needs. Each works under a written agreement and our instructions.

  1. Ticket Tailor, which issues tickets and runs check in.
  2. Stripe, which takes card payments.
  3. GoHighLevel, our customer relationship and email platform.
  4. Cloudflare, which hosts this website and receives the forms.
  5. Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snapchat and Microsoft, for measurement and advertising, only if you accept marketing cookies.
  6. Spotify, only if you press Play on our playlist. Spotify then applies its own privacy policy.

Partners and exhibitors. We share your details with a partner or exhibitor only when you ask us to: when you tick the introduction box, book an appointment with an exhibitor on Ghana Day, or ask for travel help from Adansi Travels. From that point the partner uses your details under its own privacy notice. If you win the Mefie hamper draw, we share your name and delivery details with Mefie so that the prize reaches you.

When the law requires it. We may disclose details to the police, a regulator or a court where the law requires us to.

5. Outside the UK

Some of our providers store data in the United States and the European Union. Where details leave the UK, they are protected by the UK adequacy regulations, the UK Extension to the Data Privacy Framework or the International Data Transfer Addendum, as UK law requires.

6. Cookies and measurement

Your browser stores a few small preferences: whether Comfort view is on, whether you closed the booking card, your cookie choice and your ticket selection while you book, so that you can finish later. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

  1. Without cookies, from your first visit. We count visits to improve this site: pages viewed, the country and city your connection comes from, the website or advert that brought you, your device type and how far you scroll. Cloudflare Web Analytics does this without cookies. Google Analytics runs in a cookieless mode until you choose. Nothing identifies you by name.
  2. With your permission. If you choose Accept all, Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snapchat and Microsoft Clarity may set cookies to measure our adverts and see how pages are used, including anonymised recordings of clicks and scrolling. If you choose Reject all, none of these load.
  3. When you send a form. We record the page you used, the campaign or advert that brought you, your device, language and time zone, and the country, region and city of your connection, alongside the details you give us.

Change your choice at any time with Cookie settings, also in the footer of every page.

7. How long we keep it

  1. Enquiries and form details: until 31 May 2027, then deleted, unless you booked a ticket or agreed to hear from us.
  2. Partnership, exhibitor and press correspondence: for two years after the event, so that we can invite you to future editions, then deleted.
  3. Access and dietary needs: deleted within 30 days of the event.
  4. Wall of Mothers names and draw entries: deleted by 31 May 2027. The draw winner record is kept with our accounts.
  5. News emails: your name and email until you unsubscribe.
  6. Booking and payment records: six years after the event, as UK tax law requires.
  7. Photographs and film: kept in our archive of the event, unless you ask us to remove an image of you that we have published.

8. Keeping it safe

This website runs over an encrypted connection. Payments are handled by Stripe, so card numbers never reach us. Access to our systems is limited to the people who need it and protected by strong passwords. If a breach ever put your details at risk, we would tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

9. Children

Our events welcome all ages, with children accompanied by an adult. Tickets for children are booked by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect details directly from anyone under 13, and we do not send news emails to children.

10. Your rights

  1. To be told how your details are used, which is this notice.
  2. To see a copy of your details.
  3. To have them corrected.
  4. To have them deleted.
  5. To restrict how we use them.
  6. To receive them in a portable format.
  7. To object to their use, including for direct marketing, at any time.
  8. To withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

To use any of these rights, email hello@ghanaat70uk.com or write to us at the registered office above. There is no charge, and we reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your details, please tell us first so that we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the ICO, the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes to this notice

We review this notice as the event develops. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects how we use details you have already given us, we will tell you by email.
