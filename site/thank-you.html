Fri5Mar 2027
Friday 5 March 2027

The Royal Jubilee Gala

18:30 to 00:30 · Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, London EC3V 3ND

Timings

  1. 18:30Doors and red carpet
  2. 19:45Last entry. The drums begin
  3. 20:00Seated for dinner
  4. 23:30Dancing ends
  5. 00:00The midnight toast, as Independence Day begins
  6. 00:30Venue closes

Dress code

Black Tie, ball gown or Kente. A gala dinner and ball. Ladies: a ball gown or long evening gown, or Kente if you wish. Gentlemen: black tie, or Kente worn toga style. Miniature decorations may be worn.

Bring

Your ticket QR code on your phone, and photo ID.

Nearest Tube

  • BankCentral, Northern, Waterloo and City lines and the DLRabout 2 minutes on foot
  • MonumentDistrict and Circle linesabout 5 minutes on foot
  • Liverpool StreetElizabeth line and mainline trainsabout 10 minutes on foot

Very little parking in the City. Come by Tube, train or taxi. Plan your journey with TfL

Good to know

  • Doors close at 19:45, so arrive early and enjoy the red carpet
  • No alcohol is included in the ticket. Bar for over 18s, Challenge 25
  • Two floors, connected by stairs only

Sat6Mar 2027
Saturday 6 March 2027

Ghana Day Festival

08:30 to 17:00 · Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, London EC3V 3ND

Timings

  1. 08:30Doors open. The Art Morning
  2. 08:30The Business Hub: capital, trade and booked meetings, until 14:00
  3. 09:00The exhibition opens, until 17:00
  4. 11:00The drums, then the DJ
  5. 14:00The Fashion Show
  6. 16:00Last entry
  7. 17:00Doors close

Dress code

Print and denim. Relaxed and ready to dance. A print top or shirt with jeans, Ankara, or your Black Stars shirt. Flat shoes or clean trainers. Save the ball gown for Friday.

Bring

Your ticket QR code on your phone, and photo ID.

Nearest Tube

  • BankCentral, Northern, Waterloo and City lines and the DLRabout 2 minutes on foot
  • MonumentDistrict and Circle linesabout 5 minutes on foot
  • Liverpool StreetElizabeth line and mainline trainsabout 10 minutes on foot

Very little parking in the City. Come by Tube, train or taxi. Plan your journey with TfL

Good to know

  • Seventy years to the day: Independence Day
  • Keep your wristband on, and come and go as you please
  • All ages welcome. No pushchairs or buggies. Card only bar for over 18s, Challenge 25
  • Two floors, connected by stairs only

Sunday 7 March 2027

Women of the Motherland

11:00 to 18:30 · Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, London EC3V 3ND

Timings

  1. 11:00Doors open
  2. 12:00The Market and the portrait studio
  3. 13:00Afternoon tea is served
  4. 14:00The Wall of Mothers
  5. 16:00Networking hour
  6. 18:30Close

Dress code

Sunday best. Straight from church. Lace or print, a duku headwrap, gold and beads. Families in toning colours.

Bring

Your ticket QR code on your phone, and photo ID.

Nearest Tube

  • BankCentral, Northern, Waterloo and City lines and the DLRabout 2 minutes on foot
