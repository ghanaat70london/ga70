Why it matters

A nation at seventy. A diaspora at its finest.

On 6 March 2027 Ghana reaches its Platinum Jubilee. In London, the city where Casely Hayford was called to the Bar and Kwame Nkrumah planned a free Ghana, Ghanaians and friends of Ghana gather to honour it.

A Gala on Independence Eve, toasted at midnight. The drums at 11:00 on Independence Day, seventy years to the day. A Mothering Sunday for the women who carried the nation. Three days kept with ceremony and protocol, before families, founders, faith leaders, the diplomatic community and friends of Ghana.

Partners do not sponsor an evening. They stand beside a people at the moment it remembers for a lifetime, and they are remembered with it.

We invite you to stand with us.

The audience

Who will be in the room?

Guests of every background from across the UK, Europe, North America and Ghana: diaspora professionals in finance, law, medicine, property and technology, founders and business owners, community and faith leaders, and families across three generations.

How large is the audience?

The Royal Jubilee Gala is a seated dinner at long tables, with audience figures set out in the partnership pack. The Ghana Day Festival and Women of the Motherland welcome guests through the day, and every branded moment is filmed and shared across our channels in the UK, Ghana and the wider diaspora.

Is this only for Ghanaian brands?

No. Organisations from every sector and every country are welcome. What matters is that you want to serve the Ghanaian diaspora well.

The partnership

What does a partnership include?

Each level is set out above, with what you receive before, during and after the event. See the levels.

Can more than one brand from my sector take part?

Yes. We welcome partners from every sector, and more than one brand from the same sector. Each agreement sets out how your brand is presented, so every partner has its own moment in the celebration.

Can we partner a single day?

Yes. Category partnerships such as Food or Wristband attach to a single day. See the categories.

Can we partner in kind?

Yes. Drinks, flowers, sound, light, print, travel and gifts are welcome in kind, or in a mix of cash and kind, valued and agreed in writing.

Do we receive guest data?

Only by consent. Guests who choose to meet a partner are introduced to the partners they ask to meet, by name. We never sell or hand over guest lists. This protects your brand as much as our guests.

Terms and approvals

What does it cost?

Every partnership is shaped with you. The partnership pack sets out each level, and terms are agreed in writing. Request the pack.

Who approves our logo and wording?

You do. No partner is announced until the agreement is signed and you have approved your logo and every word in writing.

Can we sample, sell or serve on the day?

Sampling and activations are agreed with each partner. Food and served drinks are subject to venue approval.

Who is behind Ghana at 70 UK?

Joint founders Asantewaa-Gloria Ayim and Adwoa Agyemang, who together opened the new Ghana Passport Office in Aldgate East. Meet the founders.

Delivery and results

When does our visibility begin?

From the day you confirm: on invitations, tickets, this website and guest emails, and in our social channels in the weeks before the doors open.

What do we receive after the event?

Photographs of every branded moment, a first report within seven days and the full partner report within thirty days, with attendance, consented introductions and reach.

How quickly can we start?

Today. Tell us your goal and a named member of the team replies within one working day with an outline. Start a conversation.
