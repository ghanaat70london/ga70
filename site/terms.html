Terms of sale at a glance

  1. All sales are final. Tickets cannot be refunded or exchanged once paid.
  2. Tickets are transferable. If you cannot attend, pass your ticket to another guest. Email us their name before 17:00 on the day before the event.
  3. Full refund only if we cancel. If we cancel an event, every ticket holder for that event is refunded the full ticket price.
  4. Bank transfer within 24 hours. Payment by bank transfer must arrive in full within 24 hours of booking, or the booking is released.
  5. All ages welcome. Children must be accompanied by an adult. The event is held over two floors joined by stairs, so pushchairs and buggies cannot be accommodated on any day.
Please read before you book

All sales are final.

  1. No refunds. Once you have paid, your ticket cannot be refunded or exchanged.
  2. Transfer it to a friend. If you cannot attend, give your ticket to someone else. Email us their name before 17:00 on the day before the event.

These terms were last updated on 21 November 2026. They form the contract between you and the organiser when you buy a ticket. Please read them before you pay.

1. Who we are

  1. Ghana at 70 UK is organised by INTEROPA Holidays Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 01190602, registered office Room 540, Linen Hall, 162 to 168 Regent Street, London W1B 5TF ("the organiser", "we").
  2. "You" means the person who buys a ticket (the lead booker) and every guest who uses a ticket.

2. Buying a ticket

  1. Prices: the Royal Jubilee Gala GBP 200 per guest. First release: GBP 150 per guest for a limited first release, up to 2 per order. Table of ten: GBP 1,800 for ten Gala guests, the price of nine (9 x GBP 200). Ghana Day Festival tickets go on sale in the New Year. Women of the Motherland tickets go on sale in the New Year. One offer applies per order: the first release and the table of ten cannot be combined with each other. Offers end when their allocation is sold. Prices include VAT and booking fees. No fee is added at checkout.
  2. Ghana at 70 UK is a series of three separate events. Each ticket is valid only for the event and date printed on it: The Royal Jubilee Gala on Friday 5 March 2027, the Ghana Day Festival on Saturday 6 March 2027, or Women of the Motherland on Sunday 7 March 2027. A ticket for one event gives no entry to the others.
  3. Your contract is formed when we confirm your booking by email after full payment is received. Until then, no place is guaranteed.
  4. Bank transfers must arrive in full, in GBP, within 24 hours of booking. Any bank or currency charges are paid by you. Unpaid places are released to other guests without further notice.
  5. Offers are limited, apply only while stated and cannot be applied to a booking already made.
  6. If a price is shown in obvious error, we may cancel the booking and refund in full, or offer the correct price.
  7. The lead booker is responsible for every ticket in the booking, for passing these terms to each guest and for the accuracy of the details given.
  8. We may limit the number of tickets per person, and may cancel bookings made in breach of these terms or for resale.

3. All sales are final

  1. No refunds. Owing to the nature of the event, tickets are not refundable and cannot be exchanged once payment is made. This includes, without limitation, a change of plans, illness, travel disruption, a refused visa, bad weather, late arrival, refused admission under these terms, or any other reason you are unable to attend or choose not to.
  2. No right to cancel within 14 days. Tickets are for leisure services on a specific date. Under regulation 28(1)(g) of the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, the right to cancel within 14 days does not apply.
  3. Chargebacks. If you dispute a payment with your bank or card provider for a ticket that remains valid, the tickets in that booking are cancelled and entry is refused. We will provide your bank with your booking record, including your confirmation that all sales are final, and recover the amount and any costs as a debt.

4. Transferring a ticket

  1. A ticket may be transferred to another guest at no extra cost. Email us the booking reference and the full name of the new guest before 17:00 on the day before the event. We reissue the ticket in the new name and the original ticket no longer works.
  2. The new guest holds the ticket on these terms.
  3. Tickets may not be resold for profit, offered on resale or auction sites, or used in a promotion or competition without our written consent. Tickets found to be resold in breach of this clause may be cancelled without refund.

5. If we cancel or change the event

  1. If we cancel. If we cancel an event, every ticket holder for that event receives a full refund of the ticket price paid, to the original method of payment, within 14 days of the cancellation notice.
  2. If we move the date or the venue. Your ticket is valid for the new date or venue. If you cannot attend, you may ask for a full refund of the ticket price by emailing us within 14 days of our notice. After that, the ticket stands for the new date or venue.
  3. Changes to the programme, running order, speakers, performers, menu or décor do not entitle a guest to a refund.
  4. If an event cannot take place, or must stop, because of events beyond our reasonable control, such as government restrictions, severe weather, strikes, a venue emergency or an instruction from the police or authorities, we will refund the ticket price if the event does not take place and cannot be rescheduled. If an event starts but must end early for these reasons, no refund is due.
  5. Our liability to you is limited to the ticket price you paid. We are not responsible for travel, accommodation or other costs you incur, except where these are booked through our accommodation service, which has its own terms. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or any other liability that cannot be limited by law.

6. Entry on the day

  1. Each ticket admits one guest, once, on the day shown, on scanning its personal QR code. Copied or duplicated tickets are refused, and only the first ticket scanned is admitted. Keep your ticket private.
  2. The name on the ticket may be checked against photo ID. Please bring it.
  3. All ages. All three days welcome guests of all ages. Every guest, child or adult, holds a ticket. Pushchairs and buggies cannot be accommodated, as the event is held over two floors joined by stairs. Under 18s must be with a responsible adult at all times, who is responsible for them throughout the event. The bar serves over 18s only, and we follow Challenge 25. Admission of under 18s is subject to the conditions of the venue licence.
  4. Doors close at the last entry time shown for that day. Late arrivals may not be admitted, and no refund is due.
  5. Saturday guests may leave and return wearing their wristband. Wristbands are fitted by our hosts with a lock that cannot be opened without cutting. A wristband that is cut, damaged, loose, transferred or not worn on the wrist is not valid for coming back in, and a new ticket must be bought.
  6. The dress code for each day is published on this website. Guests not dressed to the code for the Royal Jubilee Gala may be asked to change before entry.
  7. Bags may be searched. Food and drink may not be brought in. Items we judge unsafe may be refused.
  8. We may refuse entry, or ask a guest to leave, where behaviour is unsafe, abusive, intoxicated or disruptive, or breaks these terms or the rules of the venue. No refund is due.

7. Access and the venue

  1. Access. Owing to the nature of the venue, the event is held over two floors connected by stairs only. We have no space for high chairs, pushchairs or buggies on any day. If you have access needs, please call us before booking so we can tell you honestly what is possible.
  2. A staffed cloakroom is available. Items are left at your own risk, and valuables should be kept with you. Lost property is held for 30 days.
  3. Travel, visas and accommodation. Travel, flights, visas, accommodation and travel cards are arranged and paid for by each guest, exhibitor or partner, at their own cost. Where our accommodation service or Adansi Travels, our travel partner in Ghana, helps you, they do so on their own terms and fees. The organiser does not sponsor, pay for or guarantee any visa, flight or accommodation, and any letter we provide confirms your booking only. A refused visa does not entitle a refund of your ticket, which you may transfer under section 4.
  4. Photography and filming take place throughout the event. By attending, guests agree that images in which they appear may be used to promote Ghana at 70 UK.

8. Gifts and extras

  1. Gift tickets are issued in the name you give us and follow these terms.
  2. Extras are priced on request, confirmed in writing and payable in full in advance. They are final sales on the same basis as tickets.

9. Your agreement and the law

  1. Your agreement. Before payment, every lead booker confirms at checkout that all sales are final and that they have read and agree to these terms. Your confirmation, with the date and time, is recorded with your booking.
  2. If any part of these terms is found unenforceable, the rest remains in force.
  3. Questions or complaints: email us or call Asantewaa. We reply within one working day.
  4. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction.
  5. Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights.

10. The Mefie hamper draw

  1. Promoter. The draw is run by INTEROPA Holidays Ltd, the organiser named in section 1. The prize is given by Mefie.
  2. The prize. One hamper from Mefie. Mefie confirms the contents before the draw. There is no cash alternative. If the hamper becomes unavailable, a prize of equal or greater value is given.
  3. Who can enter. Anyone aged 18 or over, except the Ghana at 70 UK team, staff of INTEROPA Holidays Ltd and Mefie, and their households.
  4. Two ways to enter, the same chance. Every lead booker with a paid ticket order is entered once, automatically. Anyone can enter free, with no purchase, at ghanaat70uk.com/draw. Both routes go into one draw and are treated the same.
  5. One entry per person. Each person can be entered once, whichever route they use. Extra entries are removed before the draw.
  6. Closing date. Entries close at 23:59 UK time on Sunday 28 February 2027.
  7. The draw. One winner is chosen at random from all valid entries on Saturday 6 March 2027 at the Ghana Day Festival, Banking Hall, with an independent witness. You do not need to be there to win.
  8. The winner. We tell the winner by email within 3 working days. If the winner does not reply within 14 days, a new winner is drawn. The prize can be collected at Banking Hall on 6 or 7 March 2027, or is delivered free to a UK address by 31 March 2027.
  9. Publicity. With the agreement of the winner, we publish their first name and town. The winner can ask us not to.
  10. Your details. Entry details are used only to run the draw, unless you choose to hear our news. See the privacy notice.
  11. The draw is governed by the law of England and Wales.
