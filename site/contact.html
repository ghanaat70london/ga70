Quick answers

Before you write.

How quickly will you reply?

Within one working day, Monday to Friday. Partnership and press enquiries are answered by a named member of the team.

My organisation is not Ghanaian. Can we take part?

Yes. Friends of Ghana and organisations from every sector are welcome. Tell us who you want to reach and we will advise the right way in.

We are based in Germany, the Netherlands or further away.

Very welcome. We work by video call and email, and our Plan your journey page covers visas and journeys for guests and teams.

Can we send post or deliveries to the venue?

No. Banking Hall does not accept post or deliveries for the event. Exhibitors and partners receive delivery instructions with their confirmation.

Where do I buy tickets?

Only on this website. Book here. Prices include all fees.
