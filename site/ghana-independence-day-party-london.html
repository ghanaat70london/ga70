Saturday 6 March 2027 · Seventy years to the day

Ghana Independence Day in London.

The Ghana Independence Day celebration in London for 2027 is the Ghana Day Festival: Saturday 6 March 2027, 08:30 to 17:00, at Banking Hall, London. The night before, the Royal Jubilee Gala raises the midnight toast.

The day

Seventy years to the day.

At 11:00 the whole hall stops for the drums and a reading of the declaration of independence. Then the music starts again.

Across the day: the exhibition of companies of every kind, private 10 minute appointments, the Creative Showcase, the runway, Ghana on Screen and a DJ from 11:00.

Drinks and snacks are sold at the bar, by card. Food stalls sell sealed products to take home.

The night before

Midnight, 6 March.

On Independence Eve, the Royal Jubilee Gala gathers guests in Black tie, ball gown or Kente for a Ghanaian inspired dinner, live highlife and dancing. As the clock turns to 6 March, the hall rises for the midnight toast, the very hour Ghana became free in 1957.

Make it the full weekend: Independence Day at the Ghana Day Festival, and close on Mothering Sunday at Women of the Motherland.

Quick answers

Questions people ask

Where is the Ghana Independence Day celebration in London in 2027?

At Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, London EC3V 3ND, on Saturday 6 March 2027, 08:30 to 17:00: the Ghana Day Festival by Ghana at 70 UK.

Is there a Ghana Independence evening event in London?

Yes. The Royal Jubilee Gala on the eve, Friday 5 March 2027, 18:30 to 00:30, ends with the midnight toast as Independence Day begins. GBP 200 per guest.

Is the Independence Day festival family friendly?

Yes. All ages are welcome, and every guest holds their own ticket.

Can I come and go during the day?

Yes. Your wristband lets you come and go between 08:30 and 17:00.

I live in Manchester, Birmingham or Milton Keynes. How do I get there?

Trains run to Euston, and the Northern line takes you to Bank in about 10 minutes. See journey times.

What should I wear?

Print and denim, a Black Stars shirt, Kente or the colours of the flag: red, gold and green.
