Mothering Sunday

Bring your mother. She brought you here.

She ironed the cloth. She sent money home on Friday and still had Sunday dinner on the table.

Some of our mothers live around the corner. Some came down from Manchester or up from Kent for the day. Some are at home in Ghana, waiting for the photographs.

Continue the story

On Mothering Sunday, the day after Independence Day, we honour them all: a family afternoon tea, a family portrait, and the Wall of Mothers, where every mother named by a guest is celebrated aloud.

Our symbol, Akoko Nan, is a mother who shelters and corrects her children, and never lets go. This is her day.

Daughters of the Motherland

Formidable, and this Sunday is hers.

Seven on a Sunday morning in Barking. Payroll signed, her mother called from Kumasi, Lagos or Harare, her finest wrapper pressed for church.

She built careers, homes and whole communities. She is rarely celebrated in a room built for her.

On Mothering Sunday, the day after Ghana turns seventy, Ghana opens its doors to every woman, whatever her heritage.
A family afternoon tea, at Banking Hall.

The women who carried us. The women who believed in us. The women who made a way when there seemed to be no way.

An afternoon worthy of her.

Real support, beyond the day

The Resource Village.

Trusted support partners, open throughout the afternoon. Speak privately, take information home, and leave knowing exactly where to turn.

Health
Health information for women, wellbeing checks and screening guidance
Legal
Wills, family matters and property in Britain and back home
Money
Pensions, savings and financial guidance
Safety
Confidential signposting for anyone facing abuse at home
Community
Faith groups, networks and mentoring for women

The Village and the Motherland Market sit in the two side aisles, so every family keeps its table. Support partners will be announced from January 2027. Organisations serving women can apply to join the Village, and brands can register their interest.

The Motherland Toolkit

A printed guide every guest takes home.

  1. A health checklist for every decade
  2. A money and wills checklist for Britain and back home
  3. A directory of support organisations and helplines
  4. Your pledge card and the Sister Circle details

If you or someone you know is affected by abuse at home, the National Domestic Abuse Helpline is open 24 hours on 0808 2000 247.

Before you book
Is everything confirmed?

Yes. All three days are at Banking Hall, 14 Cornhill, in the City of London. If we ever cancel, every ticket is refunded in full within 14 days. It is in our terms.

Who is behind it?

The team that produced the opening of the Ghana Passport Office in Aldgate East. Watch the film.

Is there a Mothers Day afternoon tea in London in March 2027?

Yes. Women of the Motherland is a Mothering Sunday afternoon tea on Sunday 7 March 2027 at Banking Hall, for every woman and her family. Mothering Sunday falls on 7 March in 2027.

What happens during the afternoon?

One sitting, the whole room together: afternoon tea at your table, the Wall of Mothers at 14:00, the family portrait, the Resource Village and the networking hour at 16:00.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Tick This is a gift when you book and tell us whose name goes on the ticket. A Mothering Sunday gift she will remember.

Can we sit together as a family?

Yes. Family tables will be offered when tickets go on sale in the New Year.

Is the programme confirmed?

The programme shown is the plan we are building to. Performers, speakers and timings are announced as each one is contracted, and every booked guest hears first.
